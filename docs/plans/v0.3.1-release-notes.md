**Everyone must update** (the version handshake refuses mixed crews as usual), but there is no network-format change - this release is about the boats you are NOT standing on.

## Fixed

- **Sails now stay in sync on every boat, not just the one under your feet.** Rope and sail sync only ever covered the boat you were standing on. The rest of the fleet froze at whatever trim it had when someone last stood aboard, and the drift compounded: whoever boarded a stale boat first then broadcast its wrong sail state to the whole crew as if it were real, so being right depended on boarding order. Confirmed in a 3-player session where one crew member's brig showed half its sails unfurled while the captain's showed them furled, and a full rejoin was the only cure. Now every boat in the world is watched. The captain's machine re-sends each boat's rope set on a slow cycle whenever nobody is touching it, and the moment a crewmate steps onto a boat it is corrected immediately, before they can haul anything on top of stale trim. Hauling a winch from the dock, or from a neighboring boat, now syncs too; it used to do nothing at all.
- **Someone else's shipyard edit no longer wipes your copy of their boat's sail trim.** Applying a crewmate's shipyard change rebuilds that boat's sails on your machine, and the rebuild left every rope at its factory default, on your machine only. Nothing corrected it and nothing complained, and whoever next hauled that boat's ropes broadcast the defaults to everyone as if they were the real trim. The trim is now saved before the rebuild and put back after it, and a machine whose copy was just rebuilt refuses to vouch for that boat's ropes until the restore has finished.
- **A sail trimmed just before stepping ashore could stick at the wrong position for the crew.** The confirmation packet that repairs a lost rope update was only sent while you remained on that boat. Walk onto the dock, or onto another boat, within a third of a second of letting go of the winch and it was never sent. It now goes out regardless of where you are standing.
- **Full stoves swallowed firewood.** The game ignores an insert when the stove is already full and reports nothing, but the co-op layer counted it as inserted anyway, so the two machines permanently disagreed about fuel. Inserts are now checked against what actually landed in the stove.
- **You now find out when the connection to the captain dies.** Every disconnect the mod handled needed Steam to announce it. When the transport just stops delivering (a dropped network, a crashed host, your Steam account signing in somewhere else), no announcement comes: the crew froze at their last known positions and a frozen crewmate looks identical to a stationary one. A crew member now gets a warning after 6 seconds of host silence and the session ends cleanly at 30. The thresholds come from 2.4 hours of measured session traffic, where the longest gap a healthy connection ever produced was 1.6 seconds.
- **Smaller fixes from the same playtest:** the chart kit no longer appears twice when a crewmate is charting (once on the table, once floating at their hands); the title logo no longer sails off into the distance when you open the character or friends screen underway; long Steam names no longer clip the invite button; the join loading bar's step weights now match measured join timings instead of guesses; the trade feed uses the game's font instead of a default one.

## Install

Everything here is alpha. Back up your saves.
